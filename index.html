Explainable AI Lead Scoring System

A 100% deterministic, explainable B2B sales intelligence engine designed to instantly qualify, rank, and route inbound leads without black-box hallucinations.

❌

The Black-Box Problem

Pure probabilistic ML models output unexplained probabilities. Sales reps cannot tell why a lead was rejected or what talking points to prepare.

⚖️

100% Deterministic Engine

Scores (0–100) are generated through an explicit weighted rule framework. Identical inputs will ALWAYS produce identical scores with zero variance.

✨

Controlled AI Briefing

LLMs are decoupled from scoring and used strictly to synthesize natural-language executive briefings without altering score math.

How the Scoring Pipeline Works

End-to-end deterministic evaluation pipeline

Zero Server Latency
Step 01

Lead Data Ingestion

Validates 11 key firmographic, persona, and contact attributes with defensive defaults.

Step 02

7-Pillar Rule Audit

Evaluates Job Title (20), Requirement Intent (20), Size (15), Industry (15), Source (10), Contact (10), Location (10).

Step 03

Tier & SLA Assignment

Classifies lead into Hot (≥70), Warm (40–69), or Cold (<40) with dedicated sales rep action playbooks.

Step 04

Audit Log & What-If

Persists lead in local audit history, enables CSV export, and allows live sensitivity tuning in simulator.

      Attribute Max Weight Scoring Criteria & Logic
      Job Title 20 pts • 20 pts: Founder, CEO, Owner, Director, VP (C-Level / Key Decision Maker)
      • 12 pts: Manager, Head, Lead (Department Head / Influencer)
      • 5 pts: Any other specified job title (Individual Contributor)
      • 0 pts: Field left blank
      Requirement 20 pts • 20 pts: Text contains intent keywords: CRM, software, ERP, automation, solution, demo, or pricing
      • 8 pts: Text provided without specific commercial intent keywords
      • 0 pts: No requirement specified
      Company Size 15 pts • 15 pts: 50-100 or 100-500 employees (Target mid-market sweet spot)
      • 12 pts: 500+ employees (Large enterprise)
      • 10 pts: 10-50 employees (Growing small business)
      • 5 pts: 1-10 employees (Micro team)
      • 0 pts: Not provided
      Industry 15 pts • 15 pts: IT Services, SaaS, Finance, E-commerce (High adoption ICP)
      • 7 pts: Other non-empty industry (Secondary sectors)
      • 0 pts: Not selected
      Lead Source 10 pts • 10 pts: Website or Referral (High-intent inbound)
      • 7 pts: LinkedIn or Event (Warm outreach)
      • 3 pts: Cold List or Other (Outbound / Unsolicited)
      • 0 pts: Not selected
      Contact Details 10 pts • Corporate Email (4 pts): Corporate domain email = 4 pts. Free webmail (gmail, yahoo, hotmail, example.com) = 2 pts. Missing = 0 pts.
      • Valid Phone (3 pts): 10-13 digit phone number provided = 3 pts. Missing = 0 pts.
      • Company Website (3 pts): Valid web domain provided = 3 pts. Missing = 0 pts.
      Company Location 10 pts • 10 pts: Target Tier-1 Indian tech hubs: Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Gurgaon, Noida
      • 6 pts: Any other specified city/region
      • 0 pts: Field left blank
